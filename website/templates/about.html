Our Story

Where every stitch tells a story.

Starphys Cozy Crochet was founded by SW, blending a meticulous eye for detail with a lifelong passion for handmade artistry. What began as a personal fascination with the rhythmic click of hooks has blossomed into a full-scale creative brand dedicated to high-quality, "slow-made" fashion.

In a world of mass production, we believe your wardrobe should be as unique as you are. Every item is crafted entirely by hand, ensuring that no two pieces are ever identical.

The Mesh Collection

Our signature mesh tops and dresses are designed for breathability and style, perfect for layering or making a statement at the beach.

Gender-Neutral Style

Our Ruffle and Beanie hats are crafted for both genders, focusing on universal comfort and a cozy fit for any season.

Boutique Bags

Hand-stitched bags that combine durability with the unique charm of crochet artistry.

Home Sanctuary

We go beyond fashion with our handcrafted Fluffy Mats, designed to bring a touch of handmade luxury and comfort to your living space.
